# Freebase design notes

## Product direction

Freebase starts with the directory. Search, the topic sidebar, and results share the homepage. Task collections give visitors a second way in when they want a shortlist, and Play gives games a dedicated place in the navigation.

Resource cards keep the initial scan brief. A detail page gives the free offer, limitation, best use, and source. The review status refers to provider information checked at a point in time. Unreviewed listings remain browsable, with search indexing disabled for their detail pages.

## Visual system

The interface follows the original prototype’s neutral palette, clear headings, simple card grid, and category sidebar. Manrope handles display type and DM Sans handles labels and body copy. The layout can switch between a grid and a list. On narrow screens, the sidebar becomes a category picker, and the advanced filters remain in a separate disclosure. The light theme is the default and the toggle stores a preference for future visits.

The interface uses text, initials, and line icons. The game visuals are generated from geometry and CSS.
